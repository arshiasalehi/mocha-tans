Glow Guidance

How long will my tan last?

expand_more
With proper hydration and care, your Mocha tan will last 7-10 days. We recommend moisturizing daily with our oil-free body milk to maintain the richness and ensure an even fade.

What should I wear to my appointment?

expand_more
Please wear loose, dark clothing and open-toed sandals. Avoid leggings, jeans, or bras immediately after your session to prevent any bronzer transfer or streaks during the development phase.

Can I get a tan if I have sensitive skin?

expand_more
Absolutely. Our formulas are organic, paraben-free, and dermatologically tested. We use skin-soothing ingredients like Aloe Vera and Green Tea to ensure even the most sensitive skin stays hydrated and happy.
